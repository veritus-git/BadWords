BadWords v4.1.2

Every tool built for fast dialogue rough cuts

Inspect real v4.1.2 screens across local transcription, live in-RAM silence tuning, script comparison, standalone file mode, and timeline assembly. Click any window to enlarge.

01 - Live Silence & Waveform

Instant Silence & Real-Time Cut Skipping

BadWords normalizes audio loudness automatically so -42.0 dB works consistently across microphones. Drag sliders to tune silence threshold, pause percentage, min clip duration, and keep-padding in under 5 ms in RAM.

  • Island absorption swallows coughs, breaths, and desk bumps inside dead air.
  • Bottom waveform player skips red/silence cuts live when you press Space.
  • True verbatim Faster-Whisper & Apple MLX preserves every "um", stutter, and (...) mumble.

02 - Scripted Video Workflow

Smart Script Comparison

Paste your script or import .txt, .docx, or .pdf files. A DNA-inspired aligner matches what you read against the recording so you skip clean 1:1 takes completely.

  • Automatically extracts technical words and brand names from your script to improve AI accuracy.
  • Includes Side-by-Side Comparison View and Standalone Analysis when working unscripted.

03 - Standalone & Fast Silence

Standalone File Mode & Fast Silence

No DaVinci Resolve required. Drag and drop multiple video or audio files directly into BadWords (automatic 360p proxies keep 4K/8K footage smooth), or use the Silence Detection tab to strip pauses without running speech transcription.

04 - Non-Destructive Assembly

Per-Color Cut Rules, Heatmaps & Settings

Toggle per color whether BadWords auto-cuts clips or creates colored Resolve markers as an editing heatmap. Export to FCP7 XML, FCPXML, OpenTimelineIO, or EDL.
