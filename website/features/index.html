BadWords v4.1.2

Every tool built for fast dialogue rough cuts

From verbatim local transcription and live in-RAM silence sliders to multi-NLE XML export and DaVinci Resolve heatmaps - inspect the real v4 workspace below.

01 - Local AI Speech Engine

True Verbatim Transcription

Standard dictation AI tries to clean up your speech, hiding repeated words and stutters. BadWords tunes Faster-Whisper (CUDA/CPU) and Apple Silicon MLX (~11x faster on M1-M5) for true verbatim output.

  • Captures word-level timestamps across ~100 languages with live chunked streaming.
  • Flags unintelligible mumbles as (...) so you never miss bad audio.
  • Supports Large Turbo (default), Large v3, and Medium - 100% offline after download.

02 - Live RAM Audio Engine

Instant Silence & Waveform Preview

BadWords normalizes speech loudness automatically so -42.0 dB works reliably out of the box. Tune threshold, pause percentage, min clip length, and padding with instant visual feedback.

  • Sub-5 ms recalculation in RAM - drag sliders and see cuts update immediately.
  • Island absorption swallows tiny coughs, breaths, and desk bumps inside long pauses.
  • Waveform player skips marked words and silences live during playback so you hear exact pacing.

03 - Scripted Video Workflow

Smart Script Comparison

Recording from a script? Paste text or import .txt, .docx, or .pdf files. A DNA-inspired alignment engine compares your script against the actual recording.

  • Highlights only repeated takes, mistakes, and off-script ad-libs - skip clean 1:1 lines.
  • Automatically extracts technical terms and brand names from your script to boost AI accuracy.
  • Includes Side-by-Side Comparison View to inspect your script next to the transcript.

04 - Standalone & Fast Silence

Standalone File Mode & Fast Silence

BadWords v4 works with zero NLE running. Drag and drop MP4, MOV, MKV, WAV, or MP3 files directly into the queue, or switch to the dedicated Silence Detection tab to strip dead air without AI transcription.

05 - Non-Destructive Output

Assembly Rules & Multi-NLE Export

Decide per color whether BadWords auto-cuts clips or leaves colored timeline markers as an editing heatmap. Always creates a brand-new timeline - never overwrites your source.

  • Export FCP7 XML (.xml), Apple FCPXML, OpenTimelineIO (.otio), or CMX 3600 EDL.
  • Exact NTSC rational drop-frame support (23.976, 29.97, 59.94 fps) with linked A/V clips.
