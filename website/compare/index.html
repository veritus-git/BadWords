vs Gling

No cloud queues or hour caps

Long 4K podcasts and gameplay sessions take ages to upload to cloud tools and burn through monthly quotas fast. BadWords reads local audio directly, supports Apple Silicon MLX and NVIDIA CUDA, and adds DNA-inspired script comparison.

vs Descript

Built for NLE editors, not lock-in

Descript tries to replace your entire editor behind a subscription and cloud project format. BadWords focuses purely on turning raw dialogue into a clean, frame-accurate XML/EDL or Resolve timeline so you finish in your real NLE.

vs Resolve Studio

Works on Resolve Free 21.1+ too

Even if you own Resolve Studio, BadWords catches stutters Resolve smooths over, supports script diffing, and builds colored timeline heatmaps. And on Resolve Free, BadWords gives you text-based rough cutting for $0.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast does local AI transcription run without paying for cloud GPUs?+

On an NVIDIA RTX GPU (CUDA) or Apple Silicon M1-M5 Mac (native MLX, up to ~11x faster than CPU), a 30-minute video typically transcribes in under a few minutes. On CPU-only laptops, Medium or Large Turbo still runs completely offline without internet.

Are my client videos or NDA interviews ever uploaded anywhere?+

Never. All audio extraction, Whisper inference, script diffing, and timeline assembly happen strictly on your own disk and RAM. See our Privacy Policy for details on optional anonymous telemetry.

Which NLEs can import timelines cut with BadWords?+

DaVinci Resolve (Free 21.1+, Free <=21.0, and Studio), Adobe Premiere Pro, Apple Final Cut Pro, and any NLE supporting FCP7 XML, FCPXML, OpenTimelineIO (.otio), or CMX 3600 EDL. Original media clips stay linked and untouched.
