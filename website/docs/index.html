1. Quickstart Overview

BadWords automates roughly 80% of repetitive dialogue rough cutting on your own computer. Instead of scrubbing hours of audio waveforms, you work in four clean stages:

  1. Pick a Source: Drop local video/audio files in Standalone Mode, or connect to an open DaVinci Resolve timeline and choose audio tracks.
  2. Transcribe or Fast Silence: Click Analyze to run local Whisper / MLX transcription, or switch to the Silence Detection tab to strip dead air without transcription.
  3. Review & Tune: Mark mistakes or retakes by selecting words (1-4), compare against a script (.txt/.docx/.pdf), and tune silence sliders live in RAM.
  4. Assemble or Export: Build a brand-new Resolve timeline with linked clips, or export FCP7 XML, FCPXML, OTIO, or EDL for Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro.
Non-destructive by design: BadWords never overwrites your source media or original timeline. Every Assemble creates a fresh timeline copy.

2. Installation & Setup Options

Visit the Download page, choose Windows, macOS, or Linux, and paste the one-line terminal command. No admin or sudo permissions are needed.

  • [1] Fresh Install / Update: Pick 1 to install BadWords, portable Python 3.12, FFmpeg, Whisper/MLX dependencies, and DaVinci Resolve scripts (or update an existing install while preserving saves/models).
  • [2] Repair Installation: Re-checks ABI packages (PySide6, faster_whisper, av<19, pypdf, certifi) and fixes missing files without deleting models or saves.
  • [3] Move Installation: Relocates your BadWords folder to another drive safely.
  • [4] Reset / [5] Uninstall: Cleanly wipes config/caches or removes BadWords and shortcuts completely.

3. DaVinci Resolve (Free 21.1+ & Studio)

BadWords adapts automatically to every edition of DaVinci Resolve:

  • DaVinci Resolve Free 21.1+ / 22+ (Bridge Mode): Blackmagic disabled external Python scripting and .drt exports in Free 21.1+. Open your project in Resolve, click Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords Bridge once per session, then launch BadWords from your Desktop or Start Menu. BadWords communicates over a fast Lua mailbox bridge and imports timelines as linked FCP7 XML.
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio (Any Version): Launch inside Resolve (Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords) or from Desktop (enable Preferences -> System -> General -> External scripting using: Local). Defaults to FCP7 XML (linked video+audio), with optional .drt in Settings if you need Fusion comps or Color node trees preserved.
  • DaVinci Resolve Free 21.0 and Older: Embedded script or Bridge Mode both work cleanly.

4. Welcome Screen & Standalone Files

The Welcome Screen includes two tabs at the top:

  • Transcript: Speech-to-text rough cutting with script diffing, filler flagging, and per-color cut rules.
  • Silence Detection (Fast Silence): Instant silence cutting or marker generation via FFmpeg with zero AI transcription - great for podcasts, gameplay, or music tracks.

Set Source to File to drag and drop multiple MP4, MOV, MKV, WAV, or MP3 clips into the queue without opening DaVinci Resolve.

5. AI Models & Hardware (CUDA / MLX)

Models download once (~1-5 GB) and run 100% offline forever:

  • Large Turbo (Default, ~2.5 GB): Best speed/accuracy balance across ~100 languages.
  • Large v3: Maximum accuracy for complex accents and domain vocabulary.
  • Medium: Lighter option for lower-VRAM GPUs or CPU-only laptops.

Acceleration: NVIDIA CUDA (driver 525+ required; legacy GPUs automatically fall back to CPU safely), Apple Silicon M1-M5 native Metal via mlx-whisper (up to ~11x faster than CPU), plus CPU fallback. Enable More accurate transcription on the Welcome Screen for tighter chunking on rapid retakes.

6. Transcript Editor & Color Marking

Words stream into the transcript canvas with timestamps and continuous strikethrough previews on words scheduled for removal:

  • 1 - Red: Filler words, stutters, obvious mistakes (cut by default).
  • 2 - Blue: Repeated takes and false starts.
  • 3 - Green: Close script deviations / typos (marked by default for quick review).
  • 4: Eraser - clears any manual or algorithm mark to keep the word.

Configure LMB/RMB selection modes in Settings -> Editor & Canvas: Text Selection (Notepad-style drag, default LMB), Marquee Box (rectangular drag, default RMB), or Brush. Manual marks always take priority over automatic passes. Unintelligible sections are marked (...) and Ctrl+F opens live transcript search.

7. Script Comparison & Auto-Analyze

Open the Script Comparison panel on the left sidebar:

  • Compare against a Script: Paste text or import .txt, .docx, or .pdf, then click Analyze (Compare). BadWords aligns your script against the recording so clean 1:1 lines stay untouched while deviations and retakes pop out visually. Technical terms from your script are automatically fed as hints to Whisper.
  • Side-by-Side View: Inspect your original script lines right next to your spoken transcript.
  • Standalone Analysis (No Script): Click Analyze (Standalone) to auto-detect stutters, false starts, and repeated phrases directly from audio.

8. Real-Time Silence Tuning & Fillers

Open Silence Detection on the left sidebar and enable Detect and cut silence or Detect and mark silence:

  • Auto-Normalization: Audio loudness is normalized automatically so the default -42.0 dB threshold fits most mics immediately.
  • Island Absorption: Short noises like breaths, desk bumps, or coughs trapped inside dead air are absorbed cleanly.
  • Sub-5 ms RAM Sliders: Adjust Silence threshold (dB), Pauses to remove (%), Min silence duration (s), Min clip duration (s), and Keep pause (s) with zero FFmpeg waiting.

In the Filler Words panel, edit your comma-separated filler dictionary (um, uh, like...) and toggle auto-flagging.

9. Waveform Preview & Cut Skipping

The bottom player visualizes audio peaks alongside green (kept) and dimmed/hatched (cut) zones:

  • Real-Time Cut Skipping: Press Space - playback jumps straight over cut sections so you hear the final pacing before assembling.
  • 360p Video Proxies: Generated cleanly in background after transcription so heavy 4K/8K timelines scrub smoothly.
  • Playhead Sync: Ctrl+Click any word to jump the DaVinci Resolve playhead to that exact frame.

10. Assembly Rules & Multi-NLE Export

Open Assembly Options on the right sidebar (or pin favorite toggles to the Workspace panel using star icons):

  • Per-Color Rules: Toggle scissors on any color to auto-cut on Assemble, or leave off to create native timeline markers (Timeline Heatmap).
  • Standalone Export: Click Export to write Final Cut Pro 7 XML (.xml), Apple FCPXML (.fcpxml), OpenTimelineIO (.otio), or CMX 3600 EDL (.edl) with exact NTSC rational framerates (24000/1001, 30000/1001).
  • Project Saves (.bws): Includes background autosave and crash recovery (plus embedded FCPXML fallback for Resolve Free 21.1+).

11. Settings & Keyboard Shortcuts

Press Escape to open the 9-tab Settings window (with debounced search and Basic / Advanced views):

  • General: UI language (11 languages), App Icon style, update check, Settings Export/Import/Reset, and direct log opener.
  • Appearance: Unified UI Scaling (75%-200%) with Auto OS DPI mode.
  • Editor & Canvas: Segmented vs Continuous layout, fonts, millisecond timestamps, and LMB/RMB selection modes.
  • DaVinci Resolve: Switch FCP7 XML vs Studio .drt, A/V linking, and 360p timeline proxies.
  • Shortcuts & Custom Markers: Rebind keys and create custom color markers with dedicated hotkeys.
  • Support & About / Privacy: Built-in bug report form with screenshot attachments and scrubbed logs, plus telemetry/crash-report toggles.

12. Troubleshooting & Support

  • Resolve Free 21.1+ shows "No connection": Run Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords Bridge inside Resolve once for your open project session.
  • Model download fails: Check firewall/antivirus exclusions, free space (~5 GB), and folder write permissions.
  • Older GPU / Missing CUDA DLLs: Update NVIDIA drivers to 525+. If CUDA isn't supported on your hardware, BadWords switches safely to CPU automatically.
