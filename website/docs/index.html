1. Quickstart Overview

BadWords is a local AI rough cut studio that turns raw dialogue footage into a clean, cut timeline without uploading files to the cloud. A typical session takes four steps:

  1. Pick a Source: Drop local video/audio files in Standalone Mode, or pick an open DaVinci Resolve timeline and audio tracks.
  2. Transcribe or Fast Silence: Click Analyze to run local Faster-Whisper / Apple MLX transcription, or switch to the Silence Detection tab to cut dead air immediately without AI.
  3. Review & Tune: Mark mistakes or retakes by highlighting text (keys 1-4), compare against a script, and adjust silence sliders live in RAM.
  4. Assemble or Export: Generate a brand-new non-destructive timeline inside Resolve, or export FCP7 XML, FCPXML, OTIO, or EDL for Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and other editors.
Non-destructive by design: BadWords never modifies your source media or original Resolve timeline. Every Assemble creates a new timeline copy.

2. Installation & Maintenance

BadWords uses a lightweight Rust GUI installer that works without administrator or sudo rights on Windows, macOS, and Linux. See the Download page for copyable commands.

  • Option 1 (Fresh Install / Update): Downloads BadWords code, creates an isolated Python 3.12 virtual environment, installs portable FFmpeg, and registers DaVinci Resolve scripts.
  • Option 2 (Repair): Re-verifies ABI imports (PySide6, faster_whisper, av<19, pypdf) and restores missing files without touching your saves or downloaded Whisper models.
  • Option 3-5 (Move / Reset / Uninstall): Safely relocate your install folder, wipe cleanly, or remove all files and shortcuts.

BadWords checks for updates automatically on startup (configurable in Settings -> General) and supports one-click in-app updates.

3. DaVinci Resolve Setup (Free 21.1+ & Studio)

Depending on your DaVinci Resolve edition, BadWords connects in one of three ways:

  • DaVinci Resolve Free 21.1+ / 22+ (Bridge Mode): Blackmagic disabled external Python scripting and .drt exports in Free 21.1+. Open your project in Resolve, click Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords Bridge once per session, then launch BadWords from your Desktop or Start Menu. BadWords connects automatically over the Lua mailbox bridge and assembles timelines via linked FCP7 XML.
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio (Any Version): Launch inside Resolve (Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords) or from your Desktop (set Preferences -> System -> General -> External scripting using: Local). Studio supports both FCP7 XML (default, keeps audio and video linked) and Native .drt (preserves Fusion comps and Color grades).
  • DaVinci Resolve Free 21.0 and Older: Launch via Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords or Bridge Mode.

4. Welcome Screen & Standalone Mode

The Welcome Screen offers two workspaces at the top tabs:

  • Transcript: Full speech-to-text rough cutting with script diffing, filler detection, silence sliders, and cut/marker assembly.
  • Silence Detection (Fast Silence): Instant FFmpeg silence cutting or color marking with zero AI transcription - ideal for gameplay, music, or already-clean recordings.

When Source is set to File (Standalone Mode), drag and drop one or multiple video/audio files into the File Drop Zone. BadWords probes clip frame rates and audio tracks automatically and supports queued multi-file processing.

5. AI Models & Hardware Acceleration

BadWords downloads AI models on first run and stores them locally so all future sessions work 100% offline (~1-5 GB depending on model):

  • Large Turbo (Default, ~2.5 GB): Best balance of transcription speed and verbatim accuracy across ~100 languages.
  • Large v3: Maximum accuracy for complex accents or heavy technical jargon.
  • Medium: Lighter option for lower-VRAM GPUs or CPU-only laptops.

Hardware backends: NVIDIA GPUs use CUDA automatically (driver 525+ required; older GPUs safely fall back to CPU). Apple Silicon M1-M5 Macs use native Metal via mlx-whisper (up to ~11x faster than CPU).

Enable More accurate transcription on the Welcome Screen for chunked precision mode when audio contains frequent rapid retakes.

6. Transcript Editor & Color Marking

Your dialogue streams into the central transcript canvas with timestamps (nearest second or millisecond precision in Settings) and strikethrough styling on words scheduled for cutting.

  • 1 - Red: Mistakes, stutters, filler words (cut by default).
  • 2 - Blue: Repeated takes / duplicates (cut or marked per Assembly settings).
  • 3 - Green: Script deviations / close matches (marked by default for manual check).
  • 4: Eraser - clears any manual or algorithm mark to keep the word.

Selection modes (Settings -> Editor & Canvas): Choose how Left Mouse Button and Right Mouse Button behave: Text Selection (Notepad-style drag, default LMB), Marquee Box Selection (desktop-style rectangle, default RMB), or Brush. Manual marks always override automatic algorithm passes.

Search (Ctrl+F): Opens the debounced transcript search overlay to jump directly to any phrase. Fragments marked as (...) indicate unintelligible audio for quick manual review.

7. Script Comparison & Standalone Analysis

Open the Script Comparison panel on the left sidebar:

  • With a Script: Paste text or click Import Script (.txt, .docx, .pdf), then click Analyze (Compare). A DNA-inspired sequence aligner matches your recording against the script, flagging retakes and off-script words while letting you skip clean 1:1 takes. Technical words and names from your script are also fed to Whisper to reduce typos.
  • Side-by-Side View: Click Side-by-Side View to inspect your original script lines aligned next to your spoken transcript.
  • Without a Script: Click Analyze (Standalone) to auto-detect repeated phrases, stutters, and false starts directly from the transcript.

8. Real-Time Silence Tuning & Filler Words

Open the Silence Detection panel on the left sidebar to toggle Detect and cut silence or Detect and mark silence:

  • Auto-Normalization: Audio is normalized before analysis so -42.0 dB works consistently across quiet and loud microphones.
  • Island Absorption: Short non-speech sounds (breaths, mouse clicks, desk bumps) trapped inside a pause are absorbed cleanly into the silence cut.
  • Live RAM Sliders (<5 ms): Adjust Silence threshold (dB), Pauses to remove (%), Min silence duration (s), Min clip duration (s), and Keep pause (s) with zero FFmpeg waiting.

Open the Filler Words panel to customize your comma-separated filler dictionary (e.g., um, uh, ah, like) and auto-flag matching words in one click.

9. Waveform Preview & Live Cut Skipping

The bottom player renders your audio waveform with green (kept) and red (cut) zones synchronized with every word and silence cut:

  • Live Cut Skipping: Press Space to play - BadWords automatically skips over cut zones in real time so you can audition the pacing before exporting.
  • 360p Video Proxy: In Standalone or complex Resolve timelines, a lightweight 360p background proxy lets you scrub video smoothly alongside the waveform.
  • Resolve Sync: Ctrl+Click (configurable) any word to move the DaVinci Resolve playhead to that exact frame.

10. Assembly Rules & Multi-NLE Export

Open the right-hand Assembly Options panel (and favorite star shortcuts to pin your most-used toggles to the bottom-right Workspace panel):

  • Per-Color Rules: Click the scissors icon to auto-cut a color on Assemble, or leave it off to emit colored timeline markers instead (Timeline Heatmap).
  • Linked A/V Clips: FCP7 XML assembly preserves audio-video linking in DaVinci Resolve. Studio users can optionally enable .drt in Settings -> DaVinci Resolve to retain Fusion/Color nodes.
  • Standalone Export Formats: Click Export to generate Final Cut Pro 7 XML (.xml), Apple FCPXML (.fcpxml), OpenTimelineIO (.otio), or CMX 3600 EDL (.edl) with exact drop-frame timebases (24000/1001, 30000/1001).
  • Project Saves (.bws): Save and reopen editing sessions anytime with autosave and crash recovery.

11. Settings & Keyboard Shortcuts

Press Escape or click the gear icon to open the 9-tab Settings window (includes live debounced search and Basic / Advanced views):

  • General: UI language (11 languages), App Icon theme, auto-update check, Export/Import/Reset settings, and direct log folder access.
  • Appearance: Unified UI Scaling slider (75%-200%) with Auto OS DPI mode.
  • Editor & Canvas: Segmented vs Continuous flow, font family/size, line spacing, millisecond timestamps, and LMB/RMB selection modes.
  • DaVinci Resolve: Switch between FCP7 XML and Studio .drt, track linking, and 360p timeline proxy toggle.
  • Shortcuts & Custom Markers: Rebind keys and define custom marker colors with dedicated shortcuts.
  • Support & About / Privacy: Built-in bug report form with screenshot drag/paste and automatic scrubbed log attachment, plus telemetry & crash-report toggles.

12. Troubleshooting

  • Resolve Free 21.1+ says "No connection": Make sure you launched Workspace -> Scripts -> BadWords Bridge inside DaVinci Resolve first for the current project session.
  • Model download fails or stalls: Check firewall/antivirus exclusions, free disk space (~5 GB), and folder write permissions.
  • GPU error / falling back to CPU: Update NVIDIA drivers to 525+ for CUDA 12. Older GPUs automatically switch to safe CPU mode without crashing.
  • Need help or found a bug? Use Settings -> Support & Feedback inside BadWords, open a GitHub Issue, or post on r/badwordsapp.
